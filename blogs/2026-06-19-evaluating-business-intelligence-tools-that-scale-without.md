Traditional BI tools are built for the output layer, leaving data preparation in ungoverned scripts, Excel files, and Python notebooks that become invisible failure points as more teams need access. The post offers six evaluation criteria for BI platforms that scale: full lifecycle ownership, business user accessibility, edge connectivity, governance, AI governance integration, and sustainable architecture.
