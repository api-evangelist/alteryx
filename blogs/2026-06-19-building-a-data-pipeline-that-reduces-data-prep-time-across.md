Analytics teams with Snowflake and Databricks still spend Monday mornings manually extracting data and reconciling formats in Excel, a logic-capture problem rather than a data-access one. The post presents a six-step pipeline pattern: connect sources without custom code, validate schemas on ingestion, reconcile data types, blend and transform with visible logic, schedule automated execution, and deliver formatted reports.
